# 🎵 Custom MP3 Sound Effects Folder

Drop your custom `.mp3` audio files directly into this folder! The app will automatically detect and play them. If any file is missing, the app will seamlessly fall back to its built-in sound synthesizer.

---

### Recognized Sound Filenames:

1. **💥 Cannon Fire (Combat Roll & Attacks):**
   * Filename: `cannon.mp3` or `Canon_Fire.mp3` or `cannon_fire.mp3`

2. **😱 Wilhelm Scream (Plays for ALL Skirmish Outcomes!):**
   * Filename: `wilhelm_scream.mp3` or `defeat.mp3`
   * Note: Regardless of who prevails, every skirmish resolves with the iconic Wilhelm Scream!

3. **📜 Parchment Page Turn (Setup & Navigation):**
   * Filename: `page_turn.mp3`

4. **🪙 Coin / Booty Chime (Loot Card Draws & Doubloons):**
   * Filename: `coin.mp3` or `booty.mp3`

5. **🎲 Dice Rattle (Rolling Battle Dice):**
   * Filename: `dice.mp3`

6. **🧭 Compass Dial Ticks (Needle Spinning):**
   * Filename: `spin.mp3`
